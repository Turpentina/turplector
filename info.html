Genshin Physical TCG Tracker

This site is designed for collectors of the official Genius Invokation TCG physical cards produced by Hoyoverse and Shining Soul. It includes all currently released cards, including booster cards, starter decks, and promotional cards.

For gameplay accurate information, please visit the Genshin TCG discord or the Genshin TCG subreddit .

How to use the site
  • Filter or search using the sidebar
  • Click the checkmark on each card to mark it as collected
  • Click the card itself to view details
Collection data is stored locally in your browser. It is not synced across devices. Export your collection periodically as a backup.
How to read card serial numbers

Serial numbers follow a pattern like GCG01B-T001(1).

  • The first part (before the dash) is the set code, such as GCG01B.
  • The second part (after the dash) contains card type and running number, such as T001.
  • The last number in brackets is the rarity marker, such as (1).
Card categories

Each card has a category and may have a subcategory.

  • Character
  • Equipment: Weapon, Artifact, Talent
  • Support: Companion, Location, Item
  • Event: Food, Elemental Resonance
